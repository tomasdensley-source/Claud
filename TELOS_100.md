# 🜂 The Telos 100 — what I am aiming toward

> [!info] The phenomenological companion to `[[NORTH_STAR_100]]`
> The North Star is the vault's **structural acceptance criteria** (what must be true of the build). The Telos is its **why** — what the integrated life-and-vault should *look, feel, act, present, and serve* like to inhabit. Each aim carries a feasible action: see `[[TELOS_GOALS]]`. Grounded in the Burden Ontology (Z-axioms), the Five Laws, the `[!atom]` protocol, and the six Graphy branches.

Lens: 👁 look · 🫀 feel · ⚙ act · 🪧 present · 🕯 serve · 📐 doctrine.

## A. Burden & the moral ledger — *existence is accountability*
1. 🕯 It looks like a **ledger, not a scoreboard** — load taken on is visible, and so is its repair.
2. 📐 **Knowing creates owing** — once a thing is recorded, it carries weight (O01).
3. 🫀 It feels like **carrying is honored**, not pathologized.
4. ⚙ It acts like a balance sheet — **unrepaired load surfaces until closed**.
5. 🪧 Burden presents as **lineage** — Watchers → Burdened → Bastions (O11/B11).
6. 📐 Moral life reads as **balance-and-repair across an open ledger**, not rule-compliance (Z5).
7. 🕯 It makes **transferred load traceable** — what I pushed onto a later time is named.
8. 🫀 It feels weighty but **not crushing** — the system shares the load by holding it.
9. ⚙ It acts like **inheritance** — carried weight can be received and passed on.
10. 🪧 It presents **the cost first, the achievement second**.

## B. Truth, honesty & audit
11. 📐 Truth presents as **respect** — contradictions named, not buried (Z2).
12. 👁 It looks **honest** — uncertainty is visible, not hidden.
13. ⚙ It acts by **auditing, not soothing** — "audit beats catharsis" (Z14).
14. 🫀 It feels **safe to record hard news**.
15. 🪧 **Honest reporting** is built into every pass (Law 5).
16. 📐 **Evidence, interpretation, speculation never blur** (Law 4).
17. 🕯 It serves correction — **being wrong is cheap to fix** here.
18. 👁 **Provenance is always one click away**.
19. ⚙ It acts **deterministically** — the same input lands in the same home (Law 1).
20. 🪧 Truth-telling presents as **kindness, not cruelty** — respect, framed.

## C. Shame → guilt → repair
21. 🕯 **No decision is ever grounded in shame** (Z3).
22. 🫀 It feels like **guilt-that-repairs, never shame-that-hides** (Z4).
23. ⚙ It acts toward **repair over image**.
24. 🪧 Mistakes present as **correctable acts, not identity verdicts**.
25. 👁 It looks like a place where **the audit can happen** — nothing concealed by design (except the encrypted compartment).
26. 🫀 It feels **invitational** — the system never punishes.
27. 🕯 It serves **return** — re-entry after a lapse is one tap, guilt-free.
28. 📐 **Repair is logged as a first-class event**, like capture.
29. ⚙ It acts to **surface what's avoided, gently**.
30. 🪧 **Shame's hiding is made visible as a failure mode**, not a state to dwell in.

## D. Witness & obligation
31. 🕯 **To witness is to take on the obligation of carrying forward** (Z24).
32. ⚙ **Awareness reorganizes action** — once seen, it's tracked (Z11).
33. 🪧 The **Marriage** branch presents as **witness-function, never a collection counter**.
34. 🫀 It feels like **presence is the unit** — being-with, not logging-of.
35. 👁 What was seen looks **carried, not filed-and-forgotten**.
36. 📐 The **disproportionate cost** of witnessing is acknowledged.
37. 🕯 It serves **fidelity** — the absent aren't asked to bear what only the present can.
38. ⚙ Obligations **act like links, not guilt** — they resolve to a next action.
39. 🪧 **Convergence-of-witness presents as warrant** (independent corroboration).
40. 🫀 It feels like **memory honored** — nothing witnessed quietly disappears.

## E. The Descent / individuation — *Liber Novus Densley*
41. 🕯 It serves the **descent** — Jungian individuation honored, not sanitized.
42. 👁 The **shadow looks integrated, not displayed** — darkness made conscious, not performed.
43. ⚙ It acts as a **container, not a stage** — depth-work has a held, bounded space.
44. 🪧 Graphy presents **only the rhythm of attending** the descent — contents stay in the Monolith.
45. 🫀 It feels **reverent and safe** — the interior is protected (compartmentalized).
46. 🕯 **Refusals are recorded as data, not failures.**
47. 📐 **Convergence across sealed traditions is the warrant** for what's learned.
48. 👁 The **nine plant-teachers** present as containers, indexed and contextualized.
49. ⚙ It acts to **integrate, not collect** — figures move met → dialogued → integrated.
50. 🫀 It feels like **scars are kept, not erased** — "never keep a scar" is the failure mode.

## F. The six branches & presence
51. 🪧 The **whole life presents across its six real axes**, not a task list.
52. ⚙ It acts on **"last attendance," not achievement totals**.
53. 🫀 **Rest feels permitted** — the dashboard invites today's one act.
54. 🕯 **Vocation** serves livelihood-as-discipline (Sharp House).
55. 🪧 **Articulation** presents the making — codex, songs, parables, essays.
56. ⚙ **Faith** acts as repeating practice-rhythms + keystone consults.
57. 🫀 **Body & Play** feels somatic — piano, sleep, voice attended, not optimized.
58. 👁 Each branch **looks distinct at a glance** — deterministic color/icon.
59. ⚙ **Gamified follow-through acts only where it helps**, invisible where it doesn't.
60. 🕯 The branches **compound into one apparatus** no part could be alone.

## G. Knowledge as cognitive prosthetic — *doctrine made real*
61. 📐 The **external brain is a prosthetic** — without it complex reasoning fragments (Z6).
62. 📐 **Every idea has one canonical home** (Law 1).
63. 📐 **Notes are atomic** — one assertion, ≤3 assumptions, a mandatory `[!atom]`.
64. 📐 **Merge the delta, never delete blindly** (Law 3) — info is never lost.
65. ⚙ **Capture → rate → atomize → promote** acts as a visible conveyor.
66. 🪧 **Value presents as meaning × implication** — the Yin 5★ scale.
67. 📐 **Convergence is earned, not assumed** — paths kept separate until they meet.
68. ⚙ **Promotion acts via greenlight** — AI proposes, Thomas decides.
69. 🪧 **Extraction presents a trail** — `[!extracted]` marks the source.
70. 🕯 **Primary texts serve whole** — referenced, never re-atomized.

## H. The aesthetic home — *look*
71. 👁 It looks **calm, warm, legible** — Anna-Pushkin cream & soft gold.
72. 👁 **Heading hierarchy looks distinct and consistent.**
73. 👁 **Five-star notes wear a banner** like a book cover.
74. 🪧 The **callout vocabulary** presents one consistent language (`[!atom]`/`[!review]`/`[!rating]`/`[!extracted]`/`[!burden]`/`[!repair]`/`[!scar]`).
75. 👁 The **graph looks like structure, not haze**.
76. 🫀 **Density feels tuned** for sustained reading.
77. 👁 **Icons reinforce meaning, not noise.**
78. 🪧 **Imagery is purposeful** (Graphy banners), not everywhere.
79. 🫀 It feels like **a place, not a database** — one warm, coherent voice.
80. 👁 **No visual regressions** across desktop and mobile.

## I. Embodied interaction — *act & present*
81. ⚙ It acts **mobile-first** — usable one-handed on the S24 Ultra.
82. ⚙ **Tap-toggle, pinch-zoom, quick-prompt** act as the core gestures.
83. 🪧 Prose presents **TTS-safe** — terse, high-signal, dictation-friendly.
84. 🪧 **One front door** presents on open — the README dashboard.
85. ⚙ **Capture acts frictionlessly** — a clear inbox/seed lane.
86. 🪧 **Bookmarks present the highest-traffic doors.**
87. ⚙ **Templates make the right structure the easy structure.**
88. ⚙ **Automation acts idempotently** and leaves evidence.
89. 🪧 **A review queue presents what needs a human call.**
90. 🫀 It feels **portable and durable** — plain markdown, no lock-in.

## J. Meaning, convergence & service — *serve*
91. 🕯 It serves **a life, not productivity theater**.
92. 🕯 It serves **individuation as the through-line**, integrating all branches.
93. 📐 **Convergence matures into invariants** — cross-domain agreement becomes candidate truth.
94. 🪧 **Costly choices present as the real record** — what I bear reveals what I value (Z22).
95. 🕯 It serves as a **witness-system** — what is seen is carried forward.
96. 🫀 It feels like **one coherent home** — many systems, one voice.
97. 📐 **Knowledge compounds** — the graph topology becomes the argument.
98. 🕯 It serves **return across passes** — a carry-forward ledger tracks unfinished intentions.
99. 🪧 Each pass presents a **bounded, reversible delta** with a stated Telos move.
100. 🕯 It is **finishable in passes** — no permanent half-states; one home, one voice, serving one life.
