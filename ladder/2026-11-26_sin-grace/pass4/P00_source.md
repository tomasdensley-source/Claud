## The piece, verbatim (REV-5, copied by script from L4_repairs_round4.md)

Sin is being turned away, after knowledge, from the work of one's own hand; its contrary is facing that same work as one's own, which attention alone does not do. Repentance becomes that contrary only when it arrives at that facing, and grace is that facing insofar as it is given.
