### S-001
Text: Sin is being turned away from the work of one's own hand.
Anchor: "Sin is being turned away, ... from the work of one's own hand"
- S-001.a: Sin can be a standing condition of an agent, not only a single act.
- S-001.b: A work remains the work of one's own hand after it is done, so one can stand in a relation to it later.

### S-002
Text: The being turned away that is sin comes after knowledge.
Anchor: "after knowledge"
- S-002.a: The knowledge required is knowledge that the work is one's own.
- S-002.b: Knowledge that a work is one's own can persist while one is turned away from it.

### S-003
Text: The contrary of sin is facing that same work as one's own.
Anchor: "its contrary is facing that same work as one's own"
- S-003.a: Being turned away from a work and facing it as one's own are in the same respect, so they cannot both hold of one agent toward one work at one time.
- S-003.b: Facing a work as one's own includes knowing that it is one's own.

### S-004
Text: Attention alone does not face a work as one's own.
Anchor: "which attention alone does not do"
- S-004.a: Attending to a work and acknowledging it as one's own are distinct.
- S-004.b: Facing a work as one's own requires acknowledging it, not only attending to it.

### S-005
Text: Repentance becomes the contrary of sin only when it arrives at facing the work as one's own.
Anchor: "Repentance becomes that contrary only when it arrives at that facing"
- S-005.a: Repentance can fail to arrive at facing one's work as one's own.
- S-005.b: Repentance that arrives at facing one's work as one's own is then itself that facing.

### S-006
Text: Grace is the facing of one's own work as one's own, insofar as that facing is given.
Anchor: "grace is that facing insofar as it is given"
- S-006.a: Facing one's own work as one's own can be given, at least in part.
- S-006.b: Grace names what is given in such a facing, not the giver's favor.
