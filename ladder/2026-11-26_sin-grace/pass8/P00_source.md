## The piece, verbatim (REV-16, copied by script from pass7/L4_repairs_round2.md)

Sin is remaining turned away, after knowledge, from the work of one's own hand. Its contrary is to stand facing that work as one's own and for what it is, in acknowledgment and in the deeds it rightly calls for, as far as these can be seen; attention alone never reaches that facing. Repentance contains that contrary only once it arrives at that facing, and grace is that facing insofar as it is given.
