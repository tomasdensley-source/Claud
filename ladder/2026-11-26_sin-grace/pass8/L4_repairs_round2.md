# Pass 8. Revision of REV-16 under ruling R-G (operator construction; untested until filed CX)

| Change from REV-16 | Receipt |
|---|---|
| "grace is that facing insofar as it is given" becomes "grace is the giving of that facing": grace is defined by the act of giving, not by what is received | Author ruling R-G (RULINGS.md, verbatim) |

| ID | Exact sentence |
|---|---|
| REV-17 | Sin is remaining turned away, after knowledge, from the work of one's own hand. Its contrary is to stand facing that work as one's own and for what it is, in acknowledgment and in the deeds it rightly calls for, as far as these can be seen; attention alone never reaches that facing. Repentance contains that contrary only once it arrives at that facing, and grace is the giving of that facing. |
