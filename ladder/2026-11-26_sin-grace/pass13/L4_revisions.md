| ID | Exact sentence |
|---|---|
| REV-27 | Sin is remaining turned away, after understanding, from what one has made, done or left undone. Its contrary is to stand turned toward that work, rather than away from it, as one's own and for what it is, right or wrong, in acknowledgment and in the deeds it rightly calls for, as far as these can be seen; attention alone never amounts to that standing. Grace is the free giving of that standing, and repentance is the turning toward that grace; it contains sin's contrary only once it comes to that standing. Whether sought or found, repentance implies turning toward God's creation as God's work, and toward oneself within it as a whole, though not every part of oneself can be seen. |
