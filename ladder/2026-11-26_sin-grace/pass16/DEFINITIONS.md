# Pass 16. Definitions of the remaining terms of REV-31, drawn from the works (operator construction under R-D)

| ID | Term in REV-31 | Exact sentence | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| D-24 | "His call" | God's call is what God summons a person to do or to become; to turn from it is to turn from someone who calls, not only from a circumstance. | O-0210, O-0211; O-0115, O-0116 (Jonah fleeing the invocation) |
| D-25 | "His work" | God's work is what God has made, as distinct from what a creature makes of it; each person is among it. | O-0205, O-0212; D-16 |
| D-26 | "return" | To return toward one from whom one had turned is to turn back toward him and leave that turning; it does not restore the state before the turning, for the turning has been lived through. | O-0213, O-0214; D-04 |

## The presupposition of God

| ID | Exact sentence | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| P-16 | In the passage "Sin is remaining turned away, after understanding, from what one has made, done or left undone as its author. Its contrary is to stand turned toward that work, rather than away from it, as one's own and for what it is, right or wrong, in acknowledgment and in the deeds it rightly calls for, as far as these can be seen; attention alone never amounts to that standing. Grace is the free giving of that standing, though one can also come to it with no one giving it. Repentance toward God is the return toward God, and away from one's own turning from His call or His work; whether sought or found, it is not fulfilled while it still refuses to turn toward what God has made, as God's work, or toward oneself within it as a whole, as far as these can be seen.", the sentences on sin, its contrary and grace use no term whose sense requires that God exists; only the sentence on repentance toward God does. | O-0202, O-0215 |

CX-131: D-24, D-25, D-26 STAND. Vacuity flag (Ladder 318): the tester called all three "largely definitional or analytic within their stated framework"; they are definitions of terms, used as presuppositions, not offered as findings. CX-132: P-16 STANDS.
