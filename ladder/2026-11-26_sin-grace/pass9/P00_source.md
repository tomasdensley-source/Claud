## The piece, verbatim (REV-20, copied by script from pass8/L4_repairs_round2.md)

Sin is remaining turned away, after knowledge, from the work of one's own hand. Its contrary is to stand facing that work as one's own and for what it is, in acknowledgment and in the deeds it rightly calls for, as far as these can be seen; attention alone never reaches that facing. Grace is the free giving of that facing, and repentance is the turning toward that grace; it contains sin's contrary only once it arrives at that facing.
