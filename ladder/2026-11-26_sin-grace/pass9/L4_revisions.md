# Pass 9. Revisions of REV-20 under the rulings (operator construction; untested until filed CX)

| Change from REV-20 | Receipt |
|---|---|
| REV-21: "after knowledge" becomes "after understanding" | Ruling on O-28 ("after understanding"); O-0096; A-012 (CX-055) |
| REV-22: as REV-21, and "for what it is" becomes "for what it is, right or wrong", fixing the meaning ruled on O-33 in the sentence itself | Ruling on O-33 ("yes"); CX-065 #3 |

| ID | Exact sentence |
|---|---|
| REV-21 | Sin is remaining turned away, after understanding, from the work of one's own hand. Its contrary is to stand facing that work as one's own and for what it is, in acknowledgment and in the deeds it rightly calls for, as far as these can be seen; attention alone never reaches that facing. Grace is the free giving of that facing, and repentance is the turning toward that grace; it contains sin's contrary only once it arrives at that facing. |
| REV-22 | Sin is remaining turned away, after understanding, from the work of one's own hand. Its contrary is to stand facing that work as one's own and for what it is, right or wrong, in acknowledgment and in the deeds it rightly calls for, as far as these can be seen; attention alone never reaches that facing. Grace is the free giving of that facing, and repentance is the turning toward that grace; it contains sin's contrary only once it arrives at that facing. |
