| ID | Group | Exact sentence |
|---|---|---|
| A-001 | G-001 | Where a condition is defined as an agent's being turned away from something the agent knows, the condition contrary to it is the agent's facing that same thing, under the same knowledge. |
| A-002 | G-002 | A response that presupposes a prior condition, as a remedy or an act of repair does, is not that condition's contrary: the contrary is a condition that excludes it at the same time, while the response comes after it. |
