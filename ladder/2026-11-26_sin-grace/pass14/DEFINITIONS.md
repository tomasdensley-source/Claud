# Pass 14. Definitions of the terms of REV-29, drawn from the author's works and the tested record (presuppositions of REV-29)

Status column: "prior pass" reuses a sentence that already passed CX as phrased (not rerun, §5.3.8); "new" is tested in pass 14.

| ID | Term in REV-29 | Exact sentence | Sources | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-01 | "what one has made, done or left undone as its author" | The work of one's own hand is what one has authored: what one has made or done, directly or through others acting at one's direction, or has left undone when it was one's to do; it does not include what another's free choice makes of it, and it remains one's own after it is finished. | O-0148, O-0150, O-0180; A-017v2 | prior pass (CX-093) |
| D-02 | "after understanding" | To understand a work as one's own is to recognize which work is one's own and to grasp, as far as one can see, what it is and what it does; one who suspects a work is theirs and refuses to find out is not excused by that refusal. | O-0171, O-0177, O-0183; AU-003R2, A-012 | new |
| D-03 | "turned toward", "facing" | To face something is to turn toward it rather than away from it, whether for a moment or as a standing. | O-0151, O-0153; A-013v4 | prior pass (CX-093) |
| D-04 | "remaining turned away" | To remain turned away from something is to hold a turning from it, rather than toward it, as a standing over time, and not to be in the course of turning back. | O-0170, O-0171, O-0060 | new |
| D-05 | "stand turned toward" | To stand turned toward something is to hold a turning toward it as a standing over time, which persists without continuous attention to it. | O-0157, O-0158; pass6 S-003.a | new |
| D-06 | "contrary" | The contrary of a standing is a standing that cannot hold together with it in the same subject, toward the same object, at the same time, though both can be absent. | T-001, A-002v2 | new |
| D-07 | "as one's own" | To hold a work as one's own is to acknowledge oneself as its author, inwardly or in speech, rather than to disown it or lay it on another. | O-0156, O-0176, O-0185 | new |
| D-08 | "for what it is, right or wrong" | To take a work for what it is is to take it by its character as far as one can see it, right or wrong, rather than by a flattering cover or by a condemnation of the person in place of the deed. | O-0176, O-0154 | new |
| D-09 | "acknowledgment" | Acknowledging a work is holding it as one's own and naming it for what it is. | O-0172, O-0179, O-0182, O-0185 | new |
| D-10 | "the deeds it rightly calls for" | The deeds a work of one's own rightly calls for are those that answer, in proportion, what it did that is not yet answered, to those it touched or in one's own conduct, and those that carry its good or its lesson to others; a deed that cannot be done, or that would send the cost to the innocent, is not called for. | O-0180, O-0185; A-011v6a, A-011v6b, A-009v3 | new |
| D-11 | "as far as these can be seen" | What facing a past work of one's own requires is bounded by what the one facing can know: it needs only the knowledge of the work that can be had, and, where no deed can be seen to be right, the deed that can be seen to be best. | O-0183; A-010v2 | prior pass (CX-040) |
| D-12 | "attention" | Attention is holding something in view; it can be close and sustained while one disowns the thing, distorts it, or does nothing it calls for. | O-0187; A-004, A-004-S2 | new |
| D-13 | "free giving" | A giving is free when it is not owed to the one who receives it, not compelled from the giver, and not made to secure a return. | O-0181, O-0167; AU-001R | new |
| D-14 | "the free giving of that standing" (what grace gives) | A standing turned toward one's own work can be given to one by another, at least in part, as being faced and named by another can help one come to face. | O-0172, O-0186; A-016, R24-1a | new |
| D-15 | "contains" | Repentance is a compound of separable elements, such as sorrow, resolve to amend, and the facing of what one did, so it can contain the contrary of a condition without being identical to that contrary. | O-0185; A-006 | prior pass (CX-017) |
| D-16 | "oneself within it as a whole" | One is among what God made, and one can turn toward oneself as a whole, as both what God made and what one has made of it, though not every part of oneself can be seen. | O-0174; CJ-001, R24-4b, AU-011R | new |
| D-17 | "God's creation as God's work" | Facing God's creation can mean facing what God made for what it is, or facing it also as God's work; only the second faces it as creation. | O-0173, O-0175; CJ-002R | prior pass (CX-076) |
| D-18 | "Sin" | Sin is the abandonment, after understanding, of one's own work: remaining turned away from it. | O-0170, O-0171 | new |
| D-19 | "Grace" | Grace is a free giving whose gift is a maker's standing turned toward his own work. | O-0172, O-0182, O-0186; AU-001R | new |
| D-20 | "repentance" | Repentance is the turning toward the grace that freely gives a maker the standing toward his own work. | O-0173, O-0175; AU-008, R24-2a | new |
