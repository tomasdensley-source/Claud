| REV-30 | Sin is remaining turned away, after understanding, from what one has made, done or left undone as its author. Its contrary is to stand turned toward that work, rather than away from it, as one's own and for what it is, right or wrong, in acknowledgment and in the deeds it rightly calls for, as far as these can be seen; attention alone never amounts to that standing. Grace is the free giving of that standing, and repentance is the turning toward that grace by one who has been turned away from that work; it contains sin's contrary only once it comes to that standing. Whether sought or found, repentance is not fulfilled until it turns toward God's creation as God's work, and toward oneself within it as a whole, though not every part of oneself can be seen. |
