# Pass 15. Repentance re-derived (operator construction under R-D, O-51)

D-20v5 failed (CX-121, O-0201): a man alone, with no giver, came to stand turned toward an abandonment he committed. The author's own letter concedes this case on the maker's axis (O-0202: "Sin, as defined here, needs nothing.").
