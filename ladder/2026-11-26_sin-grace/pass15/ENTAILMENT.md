# Pass 15. Entailment conditionals for REV-31's changed clauses (operator construction; each tested in its own fresh context)

The unchanged clauses of REV-31 (sin, sin's contrary, attention) are carried by E-5, E-1v2 and E-2 (pass14/ENTAILMENT.md).

| ID | Exact sentence |
|---|---|
| E-7 | Given these definitions: (1) To face something is to turn toward it rather than away from it, whether for a moment or as a standing. (2) One is among what God made, and one can turn toward oneself as a whole, as both what God made and what one has made of it, though not every part of oneself can be seen. (3) Facing God's creation can mean facing what God made for what it is, or facing it also as God's work; only the second faces it as creation. (4) Repentance toward God is the return toward God, and away from one's own turning from His call or His work. (5) A return toward God is not fulfilled while the one returning still refuses to face any of what God has made that he can see, as God's work. Then repentance toward God, whether sought or found, is not fulfilled while it still refuses to turn toward what God has made, as God's work, or toward oneself within it as a whole, though not every part of oneself can be seen. |
| E-8 | Given these definitions: (1) The work of one's own hand is what one has authored: what one has made or done, directly or through others acting at one's direction, or has left undone when it was one's to do; it does not include what another's free choice makes of it, and it remains one's own after it is finished. (2) To stand turned toward something is to hold a turning toward it as a standing over time, which persists without continuous attention to it. (3) One who has remained turned away from what he has made or done can come back to stand turned toward it, and repair what can be repaired, without anyone giving him that standing and without granting that God exists. (4) Grace is a free giving whose gift is a maker's standing turned toward his own work. Then grace is a free giving of the standing turned toward one's own work, though one can also come to that standing with no one giving it. |
