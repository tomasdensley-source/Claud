## The piece, verbatim (REV-14, copied by script from pass6/L4_repairs_round2.md)

Sin is remaining turned away, after knowledge, from the work of one's own hand. Its contrary is to stand facing that work as one's own and for what it is, in acknowledgment and in whatever deed can rightly be done; attention alone never reaches that facing. Repentance contains that contrary only once it arrives at that facing, and grace is that facing insofar as it is given.
