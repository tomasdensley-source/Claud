# Pass 7, P01 Claims extraction (REV-14)

Extractor: the operator. Construction, untested. Criterion as pass 1 (O-03); 7 claims as in pass 6. Assumptions target what REV-14 newly relies on ("rightly", "whatever deed", "never reaches") and two matters testers raised but no pass has tested (omissions; whether a work can be faced fully "for what it is").

### S-001
Text: Sin is remaining turned away from the work of one's own hand.
Anchor: "Sin is remaining turned away, ... from the work of one's own hand"
- S-001.a: The work of one's own hand includes what one left undone as well as what one did.
- S-001.b: One can remain turned away from a work one has never yet faced, not only from a work one faced and then turned from.

### S-002
Text: That remaining turned away is after knowledge.
Anchor: "after knowledge"
- S-002.a: The knowledge after which remaining turned away is sin includes knowledge of what the work is, not only that it is one's own.
- S-002.b: One can know that a work is one's own without knowing what it is, as when its consequences are hidden.

### S-003
Text: The contrary of sin is to stand facing that work as one's own and for what it is.
Anchor: "Its contrary is to stand facing that work as one's own and for what it is"
- S-003.a: One can face a work for what it is even while part of what it is remains unknown to one.
- S-003.b: A work can be faced for what it is whether it is a wrong or a good.

### S-004
Text: That facing is in acknowledgment and in whatever deed can rightly be done.
Anchor: "in acknowledgment and in whatever deed can rightly be done"
- S-004.a: What can rightly be done toward a past work is determinate enough to guide the one who faces it.
- S-004.b: Where several deeds can rightly be done, the facing requires at least one of them, not every one.

### S-005
Text: Attention alone never reaches that facing.
Anchor: "attention alone never reaches that facing"
- S-005.a: Acknowledgment is never merely a kind of attention, however close the attention.
- S-005.b: Even where the rightful deed consists in attention, the facing still requires an acknowledgment beyond it.

### S-006
Text: Repentance contains that contrary only once it arrives at that facing.
Anchor: "Repentance contains that contrary only once it arrives at that facing"
- S-006.a: Repentance can arrive at the facing and later lose it.
- S-006.b: What repentance contains beyond the facing, such as sorrow and resolve, is not itself part of sin's contrary.

### S-007
Text: Grace is that facing insofar as it is given.
Anchor: "grace is that facing insofar as it is given"
- S-007.a: A facing can be given without being any less the agent's own.
- S-007.b: The facing that grace names can be given to one who was never turned away from the work.
