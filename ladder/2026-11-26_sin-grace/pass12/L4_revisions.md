| REV-25 | Sin is remaining turned away, after understanding, from the work of one's own hand. Its contrary is grace: to stand facing that work as one's own and for what it is, right or wrong, in acknowledgment and in the deeds it rightly calls for, as far as these can be seen; attention alone never reaches that facing. Repentance is the facing of God's creation as God's work, and of oneself within it as a whole, though not every part of oneself can be seen. |
| REV-26 | Sin is remaining turned away, after understanding, from the work of one's own hand. Its contrary is grace: to stand facing that work as one's own and for what it is, right or wrong, in acknowledgment and in the deeds it rightly calls for, as far as these can be seen; attention alone never reaches that facing. Repentance is the facing of God's creation as God's work, and conscience is the facing of oneself as a whole within it, though not every part of oneself can be seen. |
