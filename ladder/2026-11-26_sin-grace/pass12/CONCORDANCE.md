## Points of conflict (OPEN; for the author, not resolved by the operator)
- C1 Grace: three definitions. REV and ruling R-G: the giving of the facing. POEM: the facing itself ("of our own creation"). NAME: the naming of what love has faced. POEM's form conflicts with R-G's "not by its receiving" unless "facing" here is God's (as in NAME, "the grace with which God faces").
- C2 What faces one's own work: REV, sin's contrary (unnamed); POEM, grace; NAME, love.
- C3 Repentance: REV and AU-008, turning toward grace, with facing God's creation as implication; POEM, the facing of God's creation itself (the identity form; tested only as precondition, which failed); NAME, turning toward God's making, accepting God's facing grace.
- C4 Conscience and oneself: POEM, conscience is the facing of oneself; record, facing oneself is an implication of repentance (AU-011R).

## Operator observation (not a verdict)
In POEM, grace, repentance and conscience are each "facing", told apart by object: one's own creation, God's creation, oneself. This matches the tested shape A-015v3 (one core, turning toward rather than away, with shape varying by object). "Love is the hand that stays" matches the held form in A-013v2 ("held as a standing"). Whether that is what the author intends is the author's to say.
