# Pass 17. Scripture definitions under ruling R-S, and the revision of REV-32

## Defined by ruling R-S (scripture); untested
| ID | Term in REV-32 | Definition by quotation | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-25s | "His work" (God's work) | God's work is what the verse names as "my work and my glory": "to bring to pass the immortality and eternal life of man." | Moses 1:39 (O-0219); the author's own example | valid by ruling R-S; untested. Supersedes D-25 for "His work" (D-25 did not fail) |

## Considered under R-S and not adopted as definitions (receipts only)
| Verse | Term | Why not a perfect definition |
|---|---|---|
| James 4:17 (O-0222) | sin | It names one form of sin, the knowing omission of good; REV-32's sin is remaining turned away from one's own work. It is a receipt for "after understanding" and "left undone". |
| 3 Nephi 9:13 (O-0223) | return, repent | It calls to return and repent; it does not say what returning is. A receipt for D-20v8 and D-26. |
| Moses 1:4 (O-0220) | His workmanship | It names creation "the workmanship of mine hands" but does not define the word; it is the source of the word REV-33 uses for creation. |
| Moses 1:33 (O-0221) | what God has made | Creation was made "for mine own purpose"; a receipt, not a definition. |
