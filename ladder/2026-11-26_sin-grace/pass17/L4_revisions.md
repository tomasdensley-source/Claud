| REV-33 | Sin is remaining turned away, after understanding, from what one has made, done or left undone as its author. Its contrary is to stand turned toward that work, rather than away from it, as one's own and for what it is, right or wrong, in acknowledgment and in the deeds it rightly calls for, as far as these can be seen; attention alone never amounts to that standing. Grace is the free giving of that standing, though one can also come to that standing with no one giving it. Repentance toward God is the return toward God, and away from one's own turning from His call or from His work, which is to bring to pass the immortality and eternal life of man; whether sought or found, it is not fulfilled while it still refuses to turn toward what God has made, as His workmanship, or toward oneself within it as a whole, as far as these can be seen. |
