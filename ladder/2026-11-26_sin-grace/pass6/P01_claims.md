# Pass 6, P01 Claims extraction (REV-11)

Extractor: the operator. Construction, untested. Criterion as pass 1 (O-03); "in acknowledgment and in deed" is its own assertion here, so this pass has 7 claims. Assumptions target what REV-11 newly relies on ("remaining", "for what it is", "in deed").

### S-001
Text: Sin is remaining turned away from the work of one's own hand.
Anchor: "Sin is remaining turned away, ... from the work of one's own hand"
- S-001.a: Remaining turned away from a work can be told apart from being partway through turning back toward it, though the two may look alike at a given moment.
- S-001.b: What is wrong in a first wrongful act can be distinguished from the sin of remaining turned away from it afterward.

### S-002
Text: The remaining turned away that is sin is after knowledge.
Anchor: "after knowledge"
- S-002.a: An agent can remain turned away from a work while the knowledge of it stays present to them.
- S-002.b: Knowledge of a work, once had, makes every later moment of remaining turned away from it a moment of sin.

### S-003
Text: The contrary of sin is to stand facing that same work as one's own and for what it is.
Anchor: "its contrary is to stand facing that same work as one's own and for what it is"
- S-003.a: A work has a character, such as being a wrong or a good, that one can acknowledge or fail to acknowledge apart from acknowledging that one did it.
- S-003.b: Acknowledging a wrong of one's own for what it is, without any deed, does not exclude remaining turned away from it.

### S-004
Text: That facing is in acknowledgment and in deed.
Anchor: "in acknowledgment and in deed"
- S-004.a: There is something one can do in deed toward a past work of one's own, even when the work itself cannot be undone.
- S-004.b: Where nothing can be repaired, facing a work in deed can consist in bearing its consequences.

### S-005
Text: Attention alone does not amount to that facing.
Anchor: "which attention alone does not do"
- S-005.a: Attention can perceive a work's character accurately without acknowledging the work as one's own.
- S-005.b: Attention to a work can be complete while no deed toward it is done.

### S-006
Text: Repentance contains that contrary only once it arrives at that facing.
Anchor: "Repentance contains that contrary only once it arrives at that facing"
- S-006.a: Repentance can arrive at a facing in deed as well as in acknowledgment.
- S-006.b: Contrition that never issues in any deed falls short of the facing that is sin's contrary.

### S-007
Text: Grace is that facing insofar as it is given.
Anchor: "grace is that facing insofar as it is given"
- S-007.a: A facing that includes deed can be given, at least in part.
- S-007.b: The given part and the achieved part of a facing can lie in different registers, such as acknowledgment given and deed achieved.
