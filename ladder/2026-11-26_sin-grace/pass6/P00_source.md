## The piece, verbatim (REV-11, copied by script from pass5/L4_repairs_round2.md)

Sin is remaining turned away, after knowledge, from the work of one's own hand; its contrary is to stand facing that same work as one's own and for what it is, in acknowledgment and in deed, which attention alone does not do. Repentance contains that contrary only once it arrives at that facing, and grace is that facing insofar as it is given.
