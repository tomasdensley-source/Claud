# Pass 5, P01 Claims extraction (REV-7)

Extractor: the operator. Construction, untested. Criterion as pass 1 (O-03). Assumptions were chosen to expose what REV-7 newly relies on ("to stand facing", "contains", "once it arrives", the scope of "after knowledge" for a standing condition), not to repeat assumptions already tested in passes 1-4.

### S-001
Text: Sin is being turned away from the work of one's own hand.
Anchor: "Sin is being turned away, ... from the work of one's own hand"
- S-001.a: One can be turned away from a work without being aware that one is turned away from it.
- S-001.b: Being turned away from a work is something an agent is answerable for, not only something that happens to the agent.

### S-002
Text: The being turned away that is sin comes after knowledge.
Anchor: "after knowledge"
- S-002.a: A turning away that began in ignorance becomes sin once it continues after knowledge.
- S-002.b: The knowledge need only have been had, not still be held, for the being turned away to count as sin.

### S-003
Text: The contrary of sin is to stand facing that same work as one's own.
Anchor: "its contrary is to stand facing that same work as one's own"
- S-003.a: Standing facing a work as one's own is a condition that persists without continuous attention to the work.
- S-003.b: Being turned away from a work and standing facing it as one's own both lie in acknowledgment, so conduct alone neither makes nor breaks either.

### S-004
Text: Attention alone does not amount to standing facing a work as one's own.
Anchor: "which attention alone does not do"
- S-004.a: Attention to a work can be sustained over time as a standing condition, not only given in moments.
- S-004.b: Acknowledgment can be withheld throughout a sustained attention to one's own work.

### S-005
Text: Repentance contains the contrary of sin only once it arrives at standing facing the work as one's own.
Anchor: "Repentance contains that contrary only once it arrives at that facing"
- S-005.a: Repentance is a process that can arrive at a condition, not only an act completed at once.
- S-005.b: The standing facing that repentance arrives at can continue after the repentance itself has ended.

### S-006
Text: Grace is the standing facing of one's own work as one's own, insofar as that facing is given.
Anchor: "grace is that facing insofar as it is given"
- S-006.a: A standing condition, and not only a single act, can be given.
- S-006.b: Whether a facing is given can be a matter of degree.
