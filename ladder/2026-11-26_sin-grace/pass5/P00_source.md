## The piece, verbatim (REV-7, copied by script from pass4/L4_repairs_round2.md)

Sin is being turned away, after knowledge, from the work of one's own hand; its contrary is to stand facing that same work as one's own, which attention alone does not do. Repentance contains that contrary only once it arrives at that facing, and grace is that facing insofar as it is given.
