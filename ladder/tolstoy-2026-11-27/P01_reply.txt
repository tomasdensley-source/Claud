S-001
CLAIM: Happy families are all alike.
ANCHOR: "Happy families are all alike"
S-001.a: Families can be identified as happy.
S-001.b: There is a basis on which all happy families can be compared as alike.

S-002
CLAIM: Every unhappy family is unhappy in its own way.
ANCHOR: "every unhappy family is unhappy in its own way"
S-002.a: Families can be identified as unhappy.
S-002.b: A family's way of being unhappy can be distinguished from the ways of other unhappy families.
