# Fragment of the Chronicle — The First Ones

Of the First Ones, some accepted the Counting of Years, that their children might inherit the earth without standing forever beneath their makers.

Some refused the Count and walked undying under the sun.

Some, after ages beyond numbering, returned to the Gate of Years and asked that time be given back to them.

The mortal founders were buried and remembered.

The immortal founders became gods because no child born within the world could remember a time before them.

The Returned taught that death could be chosen without declaring life a failure.

Others departed into the lower halls and did not return.

One of the oldest refused both the Count and the Return. He carried postponed duty to the terminal horizon, stacked tomorrow upon today, and became the King in Yellow.

Therefore the covenant distinguishes:

Death, in which the life ends and remains true.

Departure, in which the life continues elsewhere.

Erasure, in which the world denies that the life ever entered it.

And stewardship, in which a knower remains within the depths so others may continue beneath the sky.

*The god may depart.*

*The covenant may remain operative.*
