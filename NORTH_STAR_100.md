# 🧭 North Star — 100 characteristics the vault aims toward

The acceptance criteria for every build pass. 10 themes × 10. See `[[README]]` and `[[00_VAULT_MAP]]`.

## I. Structural integrity & graph health
1. **Zero dangling links** — every `[[wikilink]]` resolves to a real note.
2. **No orphans** — every note reachable from at least one MOC/index.
3. **One canonical home per idea** — no silent duplicates; only dedup-logged exceptions.
4. **Deterministic taxonomy** — numeric-prefix categories, one rule, no drift.
5. **Stable IDs** — permanent note IDs; renames preserve `former_id:` backlinks.
6. **Acyclic abstraction** — upward-abstraction links never cycle.
7. **Explicit registry roles** — lookup-lists marked non-atomic, never re-atomized.
8. **Namespaced subsystems** — Z / O-B-G-P-E / LoA IDs never collide.
9. **Graph reads as structure, not haze** — tuned forces, visible arrows, section colors.
10. **Self-documenting folders** — an `_index` MOC at every level.

## II. Atomicity & note quality
11. **One assertion per atomic note** (the `[!atom]` rule).
12. **Declared assumptions** — `presupposes` / `uses` populated.
13. **Single responsibility** — compounds subdivided before entry.
14. **Claim-first titles** — the title states the claim, not a vague topic.
15. **Source-anchored** — claims cite work / author / locus.
16. **Type-tagged** — axiom / definition / principle / theorem / example explicit.
17. **Evidence / Interpretation / Speculation kept distinct.**
18. **No filler** — every note earns its place (value · info · fidelity · utility).
19. **Reverse-implication-tested** — denying the claim must cost something.
20. **Convergence-aware** — cross-domain agreement surfaced as candidate invariants.

## III. Navigation & discoverability
21. **One front door** — the README home dashboard loads on open.
22. **A whole-vault map** linking every area.
23. **A read-first path** for newcomers (the §0N onboarding sequence).
24. **Bookmarks** for the highest-traffic entry points.
25. **Consistent MOC pattern** across folders (dataview-driven).
26. **Search-optimized** — omnisearch + tags + aliases cover synonyms.
27. **Mobile-navigable** — usable one-handed on the S24 Ultra.
28. **Predictable placement** — codex routing (§1A) tells you where anything goes.
29. **Live backlinks** — every note shows what references it.
30. **An obvious capture lane** — a clear inbox/seed for raw input.

## IV. Knowledge fidelity & sourcing
31. **Primary texts preserved whole** (`Complete_Works/`, never re-atomized).
32. **Complete works referenced, not absorbed** (the §0H step-4b rule).
33. **100% content preservation** on any reformat or consolidation.
34. **Provenance retained** — where each piece came from is recoverable.
35. **Variants kept honestly** (multiple editions clearly labeled).
36. **Attachments are rendered outputs**, not canonical source.
37. **Archives marked historical**, never silently authoritative.
38. **Stale claims flagged, not trusted.**
39. **Honest reporting** — what is missing/uncertain is stated.
40. **No fabrication** — counts, hashes, versions are real or marked unknown.

## V. Governance & doctrine (Section Zero)
41. **A written constitution** (§0) governs the vault.
42. **The Laws are explicit and self-consistent.**
43. **Read-first / write-first paths defined** (§0N / §0O).
44. **Duplication governed by rule** (§0M), applied case-by-case.
45. **Supervised change** — AI proposes, Thomas greenlights.
46. **Every pass leaves an audit trail** (CHANGELOG + reports).
47. **Dedup decisions logged** with a schema, reversible.
48. **Single source of truth for version.**
49. **A carry-forward ledger** tracks unfinished intentions across passes.
50. **Drift is detected and reconciled**, not left to fester.

## VI. Aesthetic & readability
51. **Calm, warm, legible** (Minimal + Anna Pushkin typography).
52. **Folder colors signal area** at a glance, deterministically.
53. **Distinct, consistent heading hierarchy.**
54. **TTS-friendly prose** — terse, high-signal, dictation-safe.
55. **Distraction-free reading** for long primary texts.
56. **A consistent callout vocabulary** (`[!atom]` / `[!review]` / `[!rating]` / `[!extracted]`).
57. **Icons reinforce meaning**, not noise.
58. **No visual regressions** across desktop and mobile.
59. **Imagery used purposefully** (Graphy banners), not everywhere.
60. **Density tuned for sustained reading.**

## VII. Portability, durability & safety
61. **Plain-markdown-first** — survives any tool; future-proof.
62. **Local-only by default** — minimal network surface.
63. **Sensitive content compartmentalized** and access-aware.
64. **Canonical runtime state owned by its tool** (`.graphy/` never hand-edited).
65. **Backups before destructive passes.**
66. **Reproducible bundling** — one zip in, one full zip out.
67. **Git-tracked, branch-disciplined**, every change reviewable.
68. **Secrets handled deliberately** — known location, conscious choice.
69. **Rebuildable** — derived artifacts regenerate from the vault.
70. **A recovery plan** for device loss / key compromise.

## VIII. Personal alignment & values
71. **Meaning first** — serves a life, not productivity theater.
72. **Never base a decision on shame.**
73. **Truth is a form of respect** — contradictions named, not buried.
74. **Repair over image** — guilt repairs, shame hides.
75. **Reflects the whole life** across its real axes (the six Graphy branches).
76. **Tracks presence, not just achievement.**
77. **Honors the descent / individuation work** (Jung, the Red Book).
78. **Records costly choices** — what you bear reveals what you value.
79. **Witness → obligation** — what is seen gets carried forward.
80. **Rest is allowed** — the system invites, it does not punish.

## IX. Tooling & automation
81. **Plugins serve workflows** — no dead/broken enables.
82. **Mobile-first interactions** (tap-toggle, pinch-zoom, quick-prompt).
83. **Templates make the right structure the easy structure.**
84. **Gamified follow-through where it helps** (Graphy), invisible where it doesn't.
85. **Scripts are idempotent, logged, dry-runnable.**
86. **Validators guard invariants.**
87. **Automation is auditable** — every run leaves evidence.
88. **A review queue** surfaces what needs a human call (`[!review]`).
89. **No lock-in** — everything exportable.
90. **Config is documented and reproducible**, not folklore.

## X. Growth, maintenance & evolution
91. **Grows by curation, not accumulation** — pruning is first-class.
92. **Low-rated material is flagged** for keep/delete, not hoarded.
93. **A clear extraction pipeline** — capture → rate → atomize → promote.
94. **Each pass is bounded and reversible**, with a stated delta.
95. **Scales without losing navigability** — registries absorb bulk lookup data.
96. **Conventions stable enough to trust, flexible enough to evolve.**
97. **Knowledge compounds** — convergences mature into invariants.
98. **Onboarding stays cheap** as the vault grows.
99. **Finishable in passes** — no permanent half-states.
100. **One coherent home** — many systems, one vault, one voice.
